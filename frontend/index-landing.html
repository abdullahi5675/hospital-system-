Core Features

Everything a Clinic Needs

Built to handle the full lifecycle of a hospital visit — from patient registration to discharge.

📋

Electronic Medical Records

Full patient health charts with consultation history, prescriptions, allergies, and active health problems — all in one profile.

📅

Smart Appointment Booking

Patients submit appointment requests. Admin reviews, assigns a doctor, and schedules the date and time — reducing booking errors.

🛏️

Real-Time Bed Management

Track ward bed availability across all rooms. Doctors and Admins can allocate and discharge patients directly from the EMR profile.

👨‍⚕️

Doctor Management

Maintain a full doctor roster with specialisation and experience. Admins can add, edit, or deactivate doctors from the dashboard.

💊

Prescriptions & Consultations

Doctors log diagnoses, treatments, and issue prescriptions directly into the patient record during or after consultation.

🔐

Role-Based Access Control

Three distinct roles — Admin, Doctor, Patient — each with their own dashboard, permissions, and restricted data views.

🌙

Dark / Light Mode

Fully themed interface with smooth dark and light mode toggle, preserving preference across sessions.

📊

Admin Command Center

Live metrics for total doctors, patients, bed occupancy, and pending appointments — all visible at a glance.

🏨

Ward & Room Setup

Admins can create wards, rooms, and define bed counts. Beds are auto-labelled and immediately tracked in the system.

Who Uses It

Three Roles, One Platform

Each user role has a dedicated portal tailored to their daily responsibilities.

🧑‍💼

Administrator

Full System Access
  • Manage the complete doctor roster
  • Register and manage patient accounts
  • Review appointment requests and assign doctors
  • Schedule appointment dates and times
  • Configure wards, rooms, and beds
  • Allocate beds to admitted patients
  • View clinic-wide statistics and metrics
👨‍⚕️

Doctor

Clinical Access
  • View all assigned appointments
  • Approve, complete, or reject appointments
  • Access full patient EMR records
  • Log consultation notes and diagnoses
  • Issue prescriptions and log allergies
  • Allocate beds when patient needs admission
  • Discharge patients from beds
🧑‍🤝‍🧑

Patient

Personal Portal
  • Self-register and create an account
  • Submit appointment requests
  • View assigned doctor and schedule
  • Track appointment status in real time
  • View personal EMR — consultations, prescriptions
  • See current bed allocation details
Appointment Workflow

How It Works

A clear, structured process from patient request to doctor consultation.

1

Patient Registers

Patient creates an account and logs into their personal portal.

2

Appointment Request

Patient submits a request describing their symptoms and appointment type.

3

Admin Reviews

Admin reads the request, assigns the right doctor, and sets the date and time.

4

Doctor Consults

Doctor sees the scheduled appointment, opens the EMR, and begins the consultation.

5

Record Updated

Diagnosis, prescriptions, and treatment are logged into the patient's permanent record.
